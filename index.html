Frequently asked questions

How do I purchase items?

Browse our collection, add items to cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll receive payment details via WhatsApp.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping times vary by seller and location. Details are provided after purchase confirmation.

Can I return items?

Returns depend on the seller's policy. Contact the seller via WhatsApp for return inquiries.

How do I become a seller?

Click Account, sign up as a seller, and start uploading your vintage pieces with photos from your phone.

How do I upload product photos?

In your seller dashboard, click upload and select photos directly from your device. No URLs needed!

Are my products approved before listing?

Products auto-publish when you add them. Keep descriptions and images quality-focused for best results.
